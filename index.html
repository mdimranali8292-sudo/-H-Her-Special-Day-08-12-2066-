Happy Birthday, Halima. ♡

Aaj ka din tumhare liye bahut saari chhoti-chhoti khushiyan lekar aaye. Kuch aise pal, jo tumhe bina kisi wajah ke muskurane par majboor kar dein, kuch aisi baatein jo tumhare dil ko sukoon dein, aur kuch aisi yaadein jo tum hamesha apne saath rakhna chaho.

Kabhi-kabhi zindagi bahut simple si khushiyon mein sabse khoobsurat lagti hai. Ek achha din, kisi apne ki muskurahat, mann ka sukoon, ya koi chhoti si wish jo achanak poori ho jaaye. I hope your life gives you more of these beautiful little moments.

Allah kare aane wala har saal tumhare liye aur behtar ho. Tumhe zindagi mein woh sab mile jo tumhare liye achha ho. Tumhari mehnat rang laaye, tumhare sapne dheere-dheere haqeeqat banein, aur jab bhi tum thak jao, tumhe phir se aage badhne ki himmat mile.

Agar kabhi koi din achha na ho, toh yaad rakhna ki har din ek jaisa nahi hota. Mushkilein hamesha nahi rehti, waqt badalta hai, aur kabhi-kabhi sabse khoobsurat cheezein thoda intezaar karne ke baad milti hain.

May your heart always find peace, even on the days when life feels a little difficult.

Tum hamesha aise hi muskurati raho, apna khayal rakho, aur zindagi mein aane wali achhi cheezon ko dil se mehsoos karo. Tum deserve karti ho ki tumhari zindagi mein khushiyan hon, sukoon ho, izzat ho, aur bahut saare khoobsurat lamhe hon.

Aaj tumhara birthday hai, toh aaj ke din bas itna yaad rakhna — duniya ki saari pareshaniyan thodi der ke liye side mein rakh do, khud ke liye waqt nikalo, aur dil se khush raho.

I hope the coming year brings you closer to everything your heart quietly hopes for.

Wishing you a birthday filled with warmth, happiness, little surprises, and memories that make you smile long after today.

Happy Birthday once again, Halima. Allah tumhe hamesha khush rakhe. ♡
